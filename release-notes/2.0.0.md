- The page now uses your app's shared page styles, so it matches your other pages in light, dark and high contrast. Before you update, your app must merge `ms-appx:///WinUIAppKit/Styles.xaml` from winui-app-kit v1.2.0 or later (see "Host requirements" in the README). Without it the page fails when it opens, so stay on v1.x until then.
- The subtitle now reads "More apps from Dynamic Solutions (CA).", in every language the page supports.
- If your app still has a `PromotedAppsPage.Localization.cs` adapter, delete it when you update: the page translates itself. An adapter keyed on the old subtitle would stop matching anyway.
